## 1. Overview

Deliver a local application that turns user-imported Mentopoker Texas Hold'em documents into source-linked study topics, a learning tree, and grounded natural-language search. The project owner manages priorities and progress. One person is available for approximately 12 hours per week; no target delivery date has been set, so the roadmap uses relative milestones rather than invented calendar commitments.

No separate test-infrastructure Initiative is planned. The owner confirmed that dedicated test environments, services, or data tooling do not need an independent workstream. Verification tasks remain within the product work packages and the later Test Management Plan.
